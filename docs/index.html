📚 Mangago Reading List Tracker

A Chrome extension that copies your Mangago reading lists into a Google Sheet you own.

What it does

  • When you click Want to Read, Reading or Completed on mangago.me, the extension adds or updates that manga in your spreadsheet.
  • Each row holds the title, status, last chapter and page link.
  • No duplicates. Existing rows are updated in place.

Install

  1. Download the latest version and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Click the extension icon, then Create my tracking sheet.

Your data

The extension only talks to Google Sheets, and can only see the one spreadsheet it creates for you. There is no server, no account and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
