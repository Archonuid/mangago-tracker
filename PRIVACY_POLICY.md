# Privacy Policy

Mangago Reading List Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit personal data
outside the user’s browser.

All data written by the extension is stored exclusively in the user’s own
Google Spreadsheet and is accessible only to the authenticated Google account
chosen during OAuth authorization.

The extension uses Google OAuth solely to obtain permission to read and write
to the user’s Google Sheets. No data is shared with the developer or any
third parties.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or
external scripts beyond the Google APIs required for its functionality.
